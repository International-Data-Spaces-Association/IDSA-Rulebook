# Foundational concepts of a data space

The mandatory foundational concepts and functional areas of a data space are:

- Establishing trust
- Data sharing & usage
- Data discoverability
- Sharing contract negotiation
- Decentralized Identities
- Observability

Additional value-adding services that support these main functions of a data space may include these optional functional areas:

- Vocabularies & Semantic Models 
- Application and processing services
- Marketplaces
- Data trustee and escrow services
- many more optional value-added services
