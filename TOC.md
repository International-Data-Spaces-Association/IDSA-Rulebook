# IDSA Rulebook #

## Table of contents #

Below are the primary documents in this repository; each entry links to the file and includes a one-sentence description to help readers understand the document's purpose.

- [001_Introduction.md](001_Introduction.md) — Orientation: explains the Rulebook's purpose, intended audience, and how to read its normative language.
- [002_GuidingPrinciples.md](002_GuidingPrinciples.md) — Core Principles: articulates the governance principles and values that guide design and operational choices.
- [003_WhatIsADataspace.md](003_WhatIsADataspace.md) — Concept: defines a data space, key properties, and the relationship between autonomy, agency, and intermediaries.
- [004_Layers.md](004_Layers.md) — Layer Model: describes technical, economic, and legislative layers and how responsibilities map across them.
- [005_Roles.md](005_Roles.md) — Roles Reference: explains technical, business, and governance roles and their responsibilities in a data space.
- [006_DSGA.md](006_DSGA.md) — DSGA Guidance: describes the Data Space Governance Authority role, options for instantiation, and governance responsibilities.
- [007_Decentralization.md](007_Decentralization.md) — Decentralization: rationale, design patterns, and exceptions for centralized or federated components.
- [008_Trust.md](008_Trust.md) — Trust Model: presents the runtime trust model, claim reconciliation, revocation, and failure modes.
- [009_Dataspace_Trust_Frameworks.md](009_Dataspace_Trust_Frameworks.md) — Trust Frameworks: defines Dataspace Trust Frameworks (DTFs), reconciliation rules, and evidence categories.
- [010_Planes.md](010_Planes.md) — Planes Overview: details control, data, management, and application planes and their interactions.
- [100_Functional_Requirements.md](100_Functional_Requirements.md) — Functional Requirements: lists mandatory and optional technical and operational requirements for data spaces.
- [101_Achieving_Autonomy_and_Agency.md](101_Achieving_Autonomy_and_Agency.md) — Autonomy & Agency: practical mechanisms and design considerations to preserve participant sovereignty.
- [102_Foundational_concepts_of_a_data_space.md](102_Foundational_concepts_of_a_data_space.md) — Foundations: summarises the essential functional areas and building blocks of a data space.
- [103_Establishing_Trust.md](103_Establishing_Trust.md) — Establishing Trust: attribute and claim-based processes used to form trust between participants.
- [104_Attributes_and_claims.md](104_Attributes_and_claims.md) — Attributes & Claims: guidance on self-descriptions, trust anchors, and verifiable credentials.
- [105_Policies.md](105_Policies.md) — Policies: policy types, design patterns, enforcement guidance, sensitivity classes, and segmentation examples.
- [106_Dataspace_Membership.md](106_Dataspace_Membership.md) — Membership: models, onboarding workflows, membership credentials, and revocation processes.
- [107_Identity.md](107_Identity.md) — Identity: identity system options, trade-offs, DID/VC usage, and verification practices.
- [108_Data_space_participation.md](108_Data_space_participation.md) — Participation: practical steps for joining, participating, and operational expectations for participants.
- [110_Data_sharing.md](110_Data_sharing.md) — Data Sharing: contract negotiation, execution patterns, and data transfer models.
- [114_Creating_a_data_space.md](114_Creating_a_data_space.md) — Creating a Data Space: step-by-step guidance to design and instantiate a functioning data space and DSGA.
- [115_Interoperability_in_data_spaces.md](115_Interoperability_in_data_spaces.md) — Interoperability: standards, semantic models, and implementation considerations to ensure interoperability.
- [120_DataDiscoveryServices.md](120_DataDiscoveryServices.md) — Discovery Services: catalog architectures, metadata models, and discovery/access patterns.
- [121_Observability.md](121_Observability.md) — Observability: logging, auditing, decentralized monitoring, and evidence collection for disputes.
- [122_Vocabularies.md](122_Vocabularies.md) — Vocabularies: role and management of semantic models and vocabularies for policies and data assets.
- [123_Marketplaces.md](123_Marketplaces.md) — Marketplaces: marketplace patterns, commercial integration, and governance considerations for trading data.
- [124_ProcessingServices.md](124_ProcessingServices.md) — Processing Services: guidance on confidential compute, escrow, and data processing service patterns.
- [125_Connector.md](125_Connector.md) — Connector Specification: responsibilities, interfaces, and interoperability expectations for Dataspace Connectors.
- [130_AI_Agents.md](130_AI_Agents.md) — AI Agents: considerations for autonomous agents interacting with data spaces and governance implications.
- [140_Decentralized_Patterns_Onboarding.md](140_Decentralized_Patterns_Onboarding.md) — Onboarding Patterns: decentralized onboarding best practices and reference patterns.
- [999_Summary_Outlook.md](999_Summary_Outlook.md) — Summary & Outlook: summarises key takeaways and outlines future work and open questions.
